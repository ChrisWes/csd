Section 01 · The reality

Most organisations don't lack ideas. They lack the system to execute them.

They struggle to turn a promising AI concept into a capability that can operate inside existing data, architecture, risk and workflow constraints. That is the execution gap.

      Section 02 · Decide what to build

      Start with the most executable valuable use case, not the most exciting one.

      The first hard choice is which business workflow deserves a 90-day production path. A suitable first use case has visible value, a bounded scope, accessible evidence, manageable risk, and an owner who can change the workflow.

      Section 03 · Design for production

      Build the operating system around the AI, not only the AI itself.

      Production readiness is created through a loop of decisions and evidence. Each stage should have a named owner and an artefact that proves the stage is complete — if either is missing, the work is not ready to move forward.
